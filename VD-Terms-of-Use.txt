2. WHAT THE APP IS
Vedute is a reference and study instrument for the etchings of Giovanni
Battista Piranesi (1720–1778). It carries a catalogue of 1,438 works in 18
series, 1,039 of them with a full-resolution reproduction, together with
titles in 21 languages, dates, places, the collections that hold the original
sheets, and tools to zoom, measure, compare and annotate the plates. The
catalogue ships inside the App and works without a connection. The App
operates no hosted service; your own notes and readings are stored on your
device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud account.

3. THE PLATES AND THE CATALOGUE
The reproductions in the App are of works in the public domain, made available
by the holding museums and by Wikimedia Commons under open-access terms. Each
plate names its source. The catalogue — titles, translations, dates, places,
series and the concordance between impressions — is editorial work compiled
by the developer from museum records and standard references; it is offered
in good faith and may contain errors, gaps and disputed attributions. Some
works are catalogued without a reproduction and some without a date; the App
says so on the sheet rather than inventing one. Measurements, scale readings
and perspective constructions are computed from the reproduction you are
looking at and from the reference you set; they describe the print, not the
building, and are only as accurate as the scan and your reference.
